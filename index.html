INTRODUCTION

Solar photovoltaic systems provide a clean and sustainable source of electrical energy. However, variations in solar irradiance and temperature continuously affect the power generated by the photovoltaic panel.

A smart solar power management system can improve energy utilization by continuously tracking the maximum power point and intelligently managing battery charging and discharging.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Conventional solar power systems may operate away from their maximum power point due to changing environmental conditions.

In addition, inefficient battery management can result in energy losses, reduced battery life and poor utilization of available solar energy.

WORKING PRINCIPLE

  1. Solar panels convert sunlight into electrical energy.
  2. Voltage and current are continuously monitored.
  3. The MPPT controller determines the operating point corresponding to maximum available power.
  4. The converter regulates the power delivered to the battery.
  5. The battery stores the available energy for later use.

MAXIMUM POWER POINT TRACKING

Purpose

MPPT continuously adjusts the operating point of the photovoltaic panel to extract maximum available power under changing environmental conditions.

Possible Algorithms

  • Perturb and Observe
  • Incremental Conductance
  • Fuzzy Logic
  • Artificial Intelligence Based MPPT

BATTERY INTEGRATION

The battery provides energy storage and allows the generated solar power to be utilized when solar generation is insufficient.

Charging Controlled charging using available PV power.
Discharging Supplies stored energy when required.
Protection Prevents overcharging and excessive discharge.
